# Shapez Mods
A collection of my mods for [Shapez 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2162800/shapez_2__Factory/).

All of the mods in this repository are open source and free to use. Most of them are available on the [Shapez 2 steam workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2162800/workshop/), with the only exception being RenderMyFluidPackageSignals.
## Beginner's Guide
I fully intend this repository to be used by newer mod developers learning how to do certain things! As a general guide for where to start, Better Immediate Stop is my simplest mod, though Signal Copy is a close second. Both are pretty different in terms of functionality, so you may benefit from taking a look at both.

My flagship mod is currently Unlimited Wait Stop. It's my biggest and most well-documented and maintained mod containing rewirers and hooks, a mod dependency, module injection, the list goes on. I do my best to keep that mod specifically as well documented as possible.

If you're looking for a mod that adds a building/platform to the game, then that's what Hybrid Stop is about. It adds a new train stop with a custom model and modules, using the Shapez Shifter pipeline.

Legally, all of my mods are licensed under the [MIT License](https://opensource.org/license/mit/). This means you can use them in your own mods as long as you give credit. Basically, don't yoink my code, but you're encouraged to sift through it and learn from it.
## Building
If you wish to build any mods yourself, there is a tutorial of how to do so on the [Shapez 2 Modding Wiki](https://shapez2.wiki.gg/wiki/Mod_Development).

Every mod is a single class library, so nothing too complicated. I may provide builds in this repository in the future, but for now it's much easier to just upload to the steam workshop.
